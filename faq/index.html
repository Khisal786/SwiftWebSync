The Risk-Reversal Model

What does "pay only if you like it" actually mean?

It means exact risk reversal. Instead of requiring upfront design deposits for mockups you can't test, our team builds your production-ready mobile web prototype first—100% free. We deliver a live link for you to test on your phone. If it doesn't meet your expectations, you walk away owing $0. If you love it and choose to officially launch it on your custom domain, we execute under a simple 50% deposit / 50% final launch milestone.

Are there any hidden setup fees or long-term contracts?

No hidden fees, period. There are no fees to view your custom demo preview. When you approve your site for production launch, our build fee is a simple $500 ($250 deposit, $250 completion) along with a predictable $49/month managed hosting and maintenance retainer. No binding multi-year contracts.

Who owns the domain and the code if we decide to move forward?

You retain 100% ownership of your business brand, domain name, and custom content assets. To guarantee maximum speed, security, SSL encryption, and proper technical routing across mobile carriers, Swift Web Sync handles domain administration, DNS routing, and edge hosting as part of your managed service plan.

Technical Execution & Architecture

How do the specialized features work (like QR ordering or booking systems)?

We tailor the application logic to your specific industry. For restaurants, each table gets a unique URL route (e.g., yourrestaurant.com/table/4) handling local storage cart orders dispatched via secure API. For salons and studios, we build streamlined service menus and direct inquiry flows. For contractors, we deploy lightning-fast portfolio showcases and high-ticket project quote forms.

Why use static architecture (Jekyll/Vanilla JS) instead of WordPress or Wix?

Traditional platforms are bloated with plugins, prone to security vulnerabilities, and suffer from slow mobile load times—which kills customer conversion. By using pure static architecture hosted on lightning-fast edge networks, our web apps load near-instantly on mobile networks, handle traffic spikes effortlessly, and require zero database maintenance.

Can we update our menus, services, or portfolio items ourselves?

Yes. While we handle technical maintenance and edge deployment, we provide streamlined data structures and quick support turnaround so you can update prices, swap out sold-out items, or add new portfolio projects in seconds.
