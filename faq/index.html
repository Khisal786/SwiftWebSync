The Risk-Reversal Model

What does "pay only if you like it" actually mean?

It means exact risk reversal. Instead of making you pay thousands upfront for a design mockup you can't visualize, our team builds your complete, production-ready mobile web app (whether it's a restaurant menu, contractor portfolio, or studio booking app) first. We drop a live link for you to test on your own phone. If it doesn't meet your expectations, you walk away owing nothing. You only start a flat monthly subscription if you decide to keep it live and operational.

Are there any hidden setup fees or long-term contracts?

No hidden fees, period. There are no setup costs, no design deposits, and no binding multi-year contracts. You pay a predictable, flat monthly maintenance and hosting fee only after you approve the live site.

Who owns the domain and the code if we decide to move forward?

You retain 100% ownership of your business domain, your branding assets, and your custom content. We manage the high-performance hosting, continuous security updates, and code infrastructure to ensure your site loads instantly across all US carrier networks.

Technical Execution & Architecture

How do the specialized features work (like QR ordering or booking systems)?

We tailor the application logic to your specific industry. For restaurants, each table gets a unique URL route (e.g., yourrestaurant.com/table/4) handling local storage cart orders dispatched via secure API. For salons and studios, we build streamlined service menus and direct inquiry flows. For contractors, we deploy lightning-fast portfolio showcases and high-ticket project quote forms.

Why use static architecture (Jekyll/Vanilla JS) instead of WordPress or Wix?

Traditional platforms are bloated with plugins, prone to security vulnerabilities, and suffer from slow mobile load times—which kills customer conversion. By using pure static architecture hosted on lightning-fast edge networks, our web apps load in under 0.5 seconds, handle traffic spikes effortlessly, and require zero database maintenance.

Can we update our menus, services, or portfolio items ourselves?

Yes. While we handle technical maintenance, we provide streamlined data structures and quick support turnaround so you can update prices, swap out sold-out items, or add new portfolio projects in seconds.
