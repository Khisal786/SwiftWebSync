Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when filling out our interactive demo intake forms, requesting custom web prototypes, or communicating via email or phone. This includes business names, phone numbers, contact emails, custom menu or service data, and aesthetic preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information & Third-Party Processors

The information collected is used exclusively to generate, deploy, and maintain custom web prototypes and digital systems tailored to your business. Form submissions are processed securely through Formspree, and web assets are hosted on high-performance edge infrastructure via GitHub Pages. We do not sell, rent, or trade your business data to third parties.

3. Cookies, Telemetry & Direct Communications

Our website sets no tracking cookies and utilizes standard server hosting telemetry solely to ensure browser compatibility and edge security. If you submit your contact details or phone number during intake, you explicitly consent to direct project follow-up via email or phone regarding your custom prototype.

4. Data Retention

Form submissions and client communication logs processed via Formspree and our internal project management records are retained for the duration of active client engagements and up to 12 months following initial inquiry unless a deletion request is issued.

5. Data Rights & CCPA/CPRA Compliance

Under United States privacy regulations (including CCPA/CPRA where applicable), business owners and clients reserve the right to access, update, or request complete deletion of their personal or business data from our records at any time. To issue a data deletion request, email us at contact@swiftwebsync.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are strictly designed for commercial business owners and operators. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18 years of age.

7. Contact & Updates

We may update this policy periodically to reflect operational changes. For questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, contact us directly at contact@swiftwebsync.com.
