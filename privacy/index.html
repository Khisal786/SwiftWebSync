Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when filling out our interactive demo intake forms, requesting custom web prototypes, or communicating via email. This includes business names, contact details, custom menu or service data, and aesthetic preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used exclusively to generate, deploy, and maintain custom web prototypes and digital systems tailored to your business. We do not sell, rent, or trade your business data or personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies & Analytics

Our website utilizes lightweight performance tracking and standard hosting analytics to ensure optimal browser rendering, mobile compatibility, and security across visitor sessions.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption, secure forms (via Formspree), and encrypted hosting environments (HTTPS/SSL) to protect your business assets and personal communications.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy practices, please reach out to us at contact@swiftwebsync.com.
